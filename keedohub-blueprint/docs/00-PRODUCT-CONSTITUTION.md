# Product Constitution

## Identity

Keedohub is a **Creative Operating System**. The workspace is the product's center of gravity.

## What Keedohub is not

- Not an AI chatbot.
- Not an AI wrapper.
- Not primarily a portfolio/agency website.
- Not only a client portal.
- Not a marketplace with creative profiles.
- Not a collection of disconnected dashboards.

## Product thesis

Keedohub gives creative people a persistent environment in which their identity, assets, projects, releases, content, services and history accumulate.

## Human-designed and programmed

The product is deliberately designed and programmed. Engines use structured inputs, rules, states, templates, workflows and validation. AI can augment selected functions but must remain subordinate to product logic.

## Account principle

An account exists because a user needs persistent creative memory: identity, assets, projects, releases, content, history, preferences and service relationships. Public exploration does not require registration.

## Studio principle

Keedohub Studio provides human creative production inside the workspace. A request should inherit context from the user's workspace and return completed assets into that same environment.

## Feature test

Every feature must define:
- user/problem
- trigger
- inputs
- rules
- outputs
- state transitions
- permissions
- failure states
- measurable outcome
- acceptance criteria

## Brand

Retain Keedohub's established black foundation, red primary, warm gold accent and technical-premium visual language.

## Scope

Artist OS is the first major proof because the music release/content continuity problem is concrete. Other OS modules reuse Core and Engine primitives rather than becoming separate products.
