# Product Phases

## Phase 0 — Blueprint
Constitution, requirements, architecture, design system and data model.

## Phase 1 — Core
Auth, workspace, identity, assets, projects, navigation, search and notifications.

## Phase 2 — Artist OS
Artist profile, music library, releases, release workspace, EPK and lyrics.

## Phase 3 — Content Engine
Content frameworks, content library, planning, gap detection and reusable assets.

## Phase 4 — Studio
Services, briefs, orders, milestones, payments, revisions, delivery and messaging.

## Phase 5 — Creative Momentum
Next Move, incomplete-work detection, upcoming requirements and useful recommendations.

## Phase 6 — Brand OS
Brand identity, products, launches, content and campaigns.

## Phase 7 — Business OS
Teams, products, operational marketing and creative requests.

## Phase 8 — Creator OS
Creator identity, content systems, opportunities and partnerships.

## Phase 9 — Intelligence
Optional AI and recommendation capabilities.
