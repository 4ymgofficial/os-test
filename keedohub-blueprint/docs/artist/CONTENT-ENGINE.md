# Artist Content Engine

## Purpose

Prevent an artist from running out of useful creative material around a release.

## Inputs

Artist identity, release, available assets, timeline, channels, content history and goals.

## Content families

Announcement  
Teaser  
Story  
Behind the song  
Lyrics  
Visual reveal  
Countdown  
Release day  
Performance  
Reaction  
Community engagement  
Post-release continuation

## Outputs

Content items, briefs, asset requirements, reusable formats and a planned sequence.

## AI

Optional. AI may assist with language or ideation, but content structures and workflow logic are Keedohub-owned systems.
