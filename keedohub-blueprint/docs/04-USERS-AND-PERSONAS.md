# Users and Personas

## Artist

Needs to manage identity, music, releases, assets, content and creative momentum.

## Brand

Needs a consistent brand system, assets, content and launch/marketing workflows.

## Business

Needs teams, products, marketing, creative requests, projects and operational continuity.

## Creator

Needs content systems, identity, assets, publishing plans and opportunities.

## Creative team

Needs briefs, projects, approvals, assets, communication, delivery and reusable context.

## Admin / Keedohub operator

Needs service operations, user support, project oversight, payments, permissions, audit history and system health.
